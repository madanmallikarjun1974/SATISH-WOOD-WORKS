SATISH INTERIOR WORLD — OWNER DASHBOARD SETUP  (one time, about 10 minutes)
============================================================================

WHAT YOU GET
  * Every visitor who fills the entry form, every detailed enquiry and every quotation is saved in a
    Google Sheet that belongs to satishinteriorworld@gmail.com.
  * A private page — your-website/owner.html — protected by a password. It shows how many customers
    registered, how many sent the detailed enquiry form, how many took ONLY a quotation, and a list
    of every customer with their phone number (tap to call / WhatsApp). You can download it all as Excel.
  * CUSTOMER REVIEWS: customers write a review (name, stars, comment) on the website. It lands in the
    "Reviews" tab of the sheet as Pending and in the dashboard under "Customer reviews". Nothing is shown to
    the public until you press Approve (or type Approved in the Status column of the sheet). Approved
    reviews then appear on the website with the customer's name. Phone numbers are never shown publicly.
  * Every lead ALSO still arrives as an e-mail at satishinteriorworld@gmail.com (subject starts with
    [NEW VISITOR], [DETAILED ENQUIRY], [QUOTATION] or [NEW REVIEW]).

STEP 1 — Create the Google Sheet
  1. Sign in to Google with satishinteriorworld@gmail.com and open https://sheets.google.com
  2. Click "Blank spreadsheet". Name it:  Satish Interior World - Leads

STEP 2 — Paste the script
  1. In that sheet click  Extensions > Apps Script.
  2. Delete the sample code, then paste ALL the text from the file  owner-setup/Code.gs
  3. On the first code line, replace PUT-YOUR-PASSWORD-HERE with the owner password you want
     (use the password you chose — a longer one is safer; never put it in the website files). Keep the quote marks.
  4. Click the Save (disk) icon.

STEP 3 — Publish it as a web app
  1. Click  Deploy > New deployment.  Click the gear icon and choose "Web app".
  2. Description: leads.   Execute as: Me.   Who has access: Anyone.
  3. Click Deploy. Google asks for permission: Authorize access > choose the account >
     "Advanced" > "Go to ... (unsafe)" > Allow.   (It is your own script — this warning is normal.)
  4. Copy the "Web app URL" (it ends with /exec).

STEP 4 — Connect the website
  1. Open js/config.js and paste the URL between the quotes:
        sheetUrl: 'https://script.google.com/macros/s/XXXXXXXX/exec',
  2. Upload the website to Netlify again (same way as before).

STEP 5 — Try it
  1. Open your live website in a new private/incognito window and fill the entry form.
  2. Open  your-website/owner.html , type the password. The visitor should be listed.
  3. FIRST TIME ONLY: the e-mail service (FormSubmit) sends ONE "activate" e-mail to
     satishinteriorworld@gmail.com after the first form is submitted. Open it and click Confirm.
     Until you do, e-mails do not arrive (the Google Sheet works regardless).

UPDATING THE SCRIPT LATER (e.g. after a new version of Code.gs)
  Paste the new Code.gs (keep your password line) > Save > Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil icon >
  Version: "New version" > Deploy.  Same Web app URL keeps working.

CHANGING THE PASSWORD LATER
  Apps Script > change the password text > Save > Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil icon >
  Version: "New version" > Deploy.  (The website itself does not need to change.)

GOOD TO KNOW
  * The owner page is  yourwebsite.netlify.app/owner  — on the SAME Netlify site as the public website, so there is
    no extra cost. It is NOT linked from any public page, is hidden from Google, and asks for the password. Bookmark it.
    (yourwebsite/owner.html opens the same page.)
  * The password is checked by Google's server, not by the website, so it cannot be read from the page source.
  * Leads are stored forever in the sheet. You can also sort / filter / share the sheet itself.
  * Before the Google Sheet is connected, owner.html runs in DEMO mode: any password opens it, and it only shows
    made-up sample customers or forms filled on that same device. Nothing private is in it. After you connect the
    Sheet (Step 4) the password is checked by Google and only the real password works.
  * Running the site on your own PC with start-server.bat also works: leads are saved in data\leads.xlsx and the
    owner password for that local copy is in data\owner-password.txt (or set the OWNER_PASSWORD variable).
