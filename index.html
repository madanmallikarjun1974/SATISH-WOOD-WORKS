The workshop

Made in Bengaluru.
Built for your walls.

Satish Interior World is a Bengaluru manufacturer, wholesaler and trader of interior woodwork, furniture and wood materials — from plywood and solid timber to laminates, veneer and MDF/HDF boards.

We design, build and install in our own workshop — so the kitchens, wardrobes, ceilings, sofas, doors and mandirs you see here are made to your measurements, not picked off a shelf. And because we work with the materials every day, we can tell you which board or timber is right for each part of your home. Whether you want the warmth of carved heritage woodwork or the clean lines of fluted modern interiors, one team looks after it from sketch to installation.
