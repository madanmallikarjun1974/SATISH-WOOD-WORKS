The workshop

Made in Abbigere.
Built for your walls.

Satish Wood Works is a Bengaluru manufacturer, wholesaler and trader of interior woodwork and furniture. We have been working from Abbigere since 2017, with roots in the trade that go back further.

We design, build and install in our own workshop — so the kitchens, wardrobes, ceilings, sofas, doors and mandirs you see here are made to your measurements, not picked off a shelf. Whether you want the warmth of carved heritage woodwork or the clean lines of fluted modern interiors, one team looks after it from sketch to installation.
